About This Tool

A guided risk assessment tool focused on Adversarial Threats based on NIST SP 800-30 Revision 1.

Adversarial Risk Focus

This tool is specifically designed to assess risks from Adversarial Threat Sources (e.g., hackers, insiders, nation-states). It follows the structure of the standard in Appendix I of NIST SP 800-30 Rev 1.

Key Assessment Factors

  • Capability: The resources and expertise available to the adversary.
  • Intent: The motivation and willingness of the adversary to attack.
  • Targeting: The degree to which the adversary is focused on specific assets or the organization.

These factors help determine the Likelihood of Initiation, which is then combined with the Likelihood of Adverse Impact and the Impact Level to calculate the overall Risk.
