About This Tool

A guided risk assessment tool focused on Adversarial Threats based on NIST SP 800-30 Revision 1.

Comprehensive Risk Methodology

This tool integrates modern cybersecurity principles with the NIST SP 800-30 framework:

  • Threat Analysis: Capability, Intent, and Targeting of the adversary.
  • Vulnerability & Likelihood: Probability of initiation and adverse impact.
  • Business Impact Analysis (BIA): Evaluates Reputation, Legal, Operational, and Safety impacts.
  • Cyber Risk Quantification (CRQ): Estimates potential financial loss for better decision-making.

These factors combine to provide a holistic view of risk, moving beyond simple heatmaps to actionable intelligence.
