Step 4: Adversary Capability

What is the level of resources and expertise available to the adversary?

Capability Levels

  • Very Low: Little/no technical expertise; uses standard tools.
  • Low: Some expertise; uses available tools.
  • Moderate: Competent; can customize tools.
  • High: Highly skilled; creates custom tools.
  • Very High: State-sponsored; advanced persistent threat.
{% elif step == 5 %}

Step 5: Adversary Intent

What is the motivation and willingness of the adversary?

Intent Levels

  • Very Low: Unlikely to target; accidental.
  • Low: Limited motivation; opportunistic.
  • Moderate: Motivated; will target if goals met.
  • High: Highly motivated; persistent targeting.
  • Very High: Extremely motivated; stops at nothing.
{% elif step == 6 %}

Step 6: Adversary Targeting

How focused is the adversary on specific assets or the organization?

Targeting Levels

  • Very Low: Broad, indiscriminate (e.g., spam).
  • Low: Sector/Industry wide, not specific org.
  • Moderate: Specific organizations or assets.
  • High: Focused on critical assets/individuals.
  • Very High: Persistent, multi-vector targeting.
{% elif step == 7 %}

Step 7: Likelihood of Initiation

Based on Capability, Intent, and Targeting, how likely is the threat to be initiated?

Likelihood of Initiation

  • Very Low: Adversary is unlikely to initiate.
  • Low: Adversary may initiate.
  • Moderate: Adversary is likely to initiate.
  • High: Adversary is highly likely to initiate.
  • Very High: Adversary is almost certain to initiate.
